# Small vs large sideways (30 Oct 2026, written before results)
User: check whether there are small and large sideways phases. Stock-level sideways = the stock's own ER20 < 0.2 at the
Close (choppy). Size = width of its 20-bar range: % (H/L - 1) and in ATR14 multiples ((H - L)/ATR).
Realistic universe stock-days (point-in-time liquidity + stale filter), 2000-01-01..2025-08-15.
A. Distribution of the width for choppy days vs trending days (ER20 >= 0.3): quantiles, shares in buckets
   (<10%, 10-20%, 20-40%, >=40%; <4, 4-6, 6-8, >=8 ATR) and a fine histogram (is it two groups or one continuum?).
B. Persistence and what follows, choppy days by % bucket: share still choppy and in the same bucket 20 bars later; next
   20-day |return| and which side breaks first within 20 bars (High > range high + 1 ATR vs Low < range low - 1 ATR).
C. Who earns where: trades entered when the stock was choppy (ER20 at the previous Close < 0.2) by width bucket (V2
   realistic, Swinger ideal): trades/year, win rate, expectancy; and P&L of position-days on choppy stocks (state at the
   previous Close) by width bucket: share of total P&L and return per day on capital.
Descriptive; no trading rule is adopted from this.
